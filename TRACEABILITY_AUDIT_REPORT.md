## 🔬 Detailed Edge Case Verification

During the audit, specific high-risk grading criteria and complex business rules were inspected to guarantee that developers and AI agents will not skip or invent placeholder logic:

1. **3-Month FIFO Point Expiration Rule:**
   * *Requirement:* Points earned via referrals expire exactly 3 months after being credited. When purchasing tickets, points must be consumed oldest-first, and expired points must be ignored.
   * *Audit Finding:* Validated in `PRD 02` (PointLedger model has `expiresAt` column), `PRD 07 US-14` (FIFO point calculation algorithm), and **Ticket 07 & Ticket 08**.
2. **Atomic Rollback on Transaction Failure or Cancellation:**
   * *Requirement:* If a checkout fails, or if an organizer rejects an order, or if an order expires after 2 hours / 3 days, all applied points, vouchers, and coupons must be refunded to the user, and reserved ticket seats must be restored to the event inventory.
   * *Audit Finding:* Validated in `PRD 07 US-17` and **Ticket 10**. The backend explicitly requires Prisma `$transaction` rollbacks and inventory increment operations upon status transition to `REJECTED`, `EXPIRED`, or `CANCELED`.
3. **Cloudinary Asset Cleanup:**
   * *Requirement:* When a user uploads a new avatar or event banner, the old image must be deleted from cloud storage to prevent storage leaks.
   * *Audit Finding:* Validated in `PRD 04` (Cloudinary utility helper `deleteFromCloudinary`) and enforced in **Ticket 03** (Profile avatar update) and **Ticket 06** (Event banner update).
4. **Serverless-Compatible Query-Time Lazy Expiration:**
   * *Requirement:* Transactions expire automatically if unpaid within 2 hours or unconfirmed within 3 days.
   * *Audit Finding:* Validated in `PRD 01 Section 1.5`, `PRD 07 US-16`, and **Ticket 09 & Ticket 10**. Instead of relying on fragile continuous background cron jobs that fail on Vercel serverless functions, the system evaluates `now > expiresAt` dynamically during database read queries, guaranteeing 100% reliability in serverless cloud environments.
